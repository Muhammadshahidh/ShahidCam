ShahidCam is an advanced, privacy-first dashcam and multimedia recording suite designed for security, automotive safety, and high-performance video capture.

KEY FEATURES:
• Intelligent Dashcam Mode: High-fidelity loop recording with automated speed and timestamp watermarking.
• Emergency Collision Detection: Real-time 3-axis accelerometer sensor locks accident footage to protect it from being overwritten.
• Smart Thermal & Battery Guard: Proactively monitors device battery temperature to prevent overheating and file corruption.
• Dual Camera Recording: Capture front and rear cameras simultaneously with customizable picture-in-picture layout (on supported hardware).
• Local Web Studio: Stream live video and monitor your camera securely over your local Wi-Fi network with zero third-party cloud dependency.
• Storage Auto-Recycler: Continuously maintains available storage by recycling only the oldest unprotected clips.
• In-App Video Editor: Trim, split, and export videos quickly with hardware-accelerated processing.
• Privacy & Biometric Lock: Protect your recordings with biometric fingerprint and PIN authentication.

100% ad-free, transparent, and built with user privacy as the top priority.
